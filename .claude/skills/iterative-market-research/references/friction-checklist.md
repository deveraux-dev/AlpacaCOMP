# Friction Checklist

Run these checks before recommending a build or writeup move.

- Judge-visible payoff vs. builder excitement
- P&L score vs. eligibility-gate checkbox
- Writeup trust vs. demo risk
- Local/bounded fix vs. platform rebuild ambition
- Compiler/engine automation vs. messy edge cases
- Demo impressiveness vs. actual scored criteria
- Stack advantage vs. remaining-hours cost
- Evidence quality vs. overclaiming
- Competitor parity vs. differentiation
- Deadline proximity vs. audience vanity
- Manual verification value vs. future automation
- Launch speed vs. proof burden

For each friction:

```markdown
FORCES_INVOLVED:
WHY_IT_MATTERS:
DEADLINE_RISK:

```
